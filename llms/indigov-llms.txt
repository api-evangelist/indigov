# Indigov

> Indigov is a constituent relationship management (CRM) platform purpose-built for
> government — U.S. Congressional offices, state legislatures, and municipal agencies.
> It unifies inbound constituent communications from email, web forms, SMS, social
> media, phone, and scanned mail into a single cross-organizational inbox, and layers
> casework tracking, constituent routing, smart tagging, and mass-email triage on top.
> Indigov serves government offices across 35 states and 2 territories and was
> acquired by Granicus, joining its Government Experience Cloud (GXC).

## Important for agents

Indigov publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API reference,
no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server, and no A2A agent card. Every
`/.well-known/` path on indigov.com, www.indigov.com and app.indigov.com returns 404.
Access to the platform is through the authenticated customer application at
app.indigov.com; integration work is arranged through the vendor, not self-serve.

Do not attempt to construct an Indigov API call. If a task requires programmatic
access to Indigov data, route the request to Indigov / Granicus sales or support.

## Products

- Indigov Platform: constituent CRM — rapid email response and mass-email triage,
  centralized casework tracking and routing, a multi-channel "inbox for everything",
  smart tagging, and a web + mobile application for field staff.
- Indigov Outreach: bulk constituent communications — templated and personalized
  messaging, branded .gov email delivery, audience building and list filtering, and
  engagement insights.
